## [1.1.0] - 2026-11-04

The first public release of the SallaPush iOS SDK, for the mobile apps of Salla stores.

### Added

- The `SallaPush` library for the app and `SallaPushExtension` for its Notification Service Extension, with Swift
  Package Manager or CocoaPods. iOS 15 or later, no dependencies, and a privacy manifest in each library.
- Customer login and logout, the notification permission, click and foreground listeners, tags, the push
  subscription, consent, logging and the app icon badge. The README shows the setup and each task, and
  `docs/REFERENCE.md` the API in detail.
- The MIT License.
